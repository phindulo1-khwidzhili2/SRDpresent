SRD GRANT REDESIGN - DEMO
=========================

Open index.html in Chrome or Edge (double-click it). No internet or server needed,
except for the font, which falls back to Arial when offline.

Every form is pre-filled with dummy data, so you only need to click:

  Apply (SA ID route)
    Landing page > "South African ID Holders" > CLICK HERE TO PROCEED
    Send me a PIN  (the PIN fills itself in)  > Verify PIN
    Accept and continue > Save and continue > Save and continue > Submit application
    > Check my status

  Check status and manage the application
    Check status > "Appeal this month" on June 2026 > Lodge appeal
    Check status > Manage my application:
      Reconfirm my details  (Yes, everything is correct)
      Update banking details (bank account, Cash Send or collect at a store)
      Appeal a declined month

  Asylum seeker / permit route
    Landing page > "Asylum Seekers and Special Permit Holders" > same steps

  Login (top right) also opens Check status.
